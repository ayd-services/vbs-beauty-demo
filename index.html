Idea worth exploring — auto-answer

When a call comes in and nobody can pick up (mid-treatment, day off), an automated assistant answers instead of it ringing out — gives opening hours and prices, and either books a slot directly or takes a message with a callback promise. Doesn't replace answering calls herself, just catches the ones that would otherwise go to voicemail or nowhere. Worth scoping properly once real missed-call volume is known — not simulated here beyond the concept.

What we'd need from you

However bookings are currently tracked (paper diary, booking app, Instagram DMs) and whether missed calls are logged anywhere at all today — likely not, which is exactly the gap this would close.
